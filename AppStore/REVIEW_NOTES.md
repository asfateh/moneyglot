2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Moneyglot is a personal income and expense tracker for people whose money lives in more than one currency: expats and immigrants, freelancers paid by foreign clients, people with a mortgage or property abroad, and frequent travelers. Most budget apps assume a single currency. Moneyglot keeps every entry (salary, mortgage, car loan, credit card, bills, insurance, everyday spending) in the currency it actually happened in and shows monthly, quarterly and yearly reports converted into whichever currency the user chooses. Recurring payments are recorded automatically. The optional Moneyglot Pro subscription adds smart insights (debt-to-income, housing costs, spending spikes, subscriptions, foreign card fees) and a personal savings plan generated on-device with Apple Intelligence.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES (no login required)
- Launch the app. On the empty Overview screen tap "Explore with sample data" to load seven months of demo entries in USD, CAD, EUR and GBP.
- Overview: this month's summary, spending by type and upcoming repeating payments.
- Activity: tap + to add an entry, tap the currency button to choose any of 160+ currencies, and use Repeat for recurring payments.
- Reports: switch between Month, Quarter and Year, and change the report currency with the currency button at the top left.
- Insights (paid): tap "Start free trial" and complete the purchase with a sandbox account. Smart insights then appear. "Create my plan" generates the AI savings plan on devices that support Apple Intelligence; if the on-device model declines a request, the app builds the plan from its smart insights and says so. On devices without Apple Intelligence the app explains that the AI plan is unavailable and still shows the smart insights.
- Settings: report currency, exchange rates, repeating payments, Manage subscription, Restore purchases, CSV export and Delete all data.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com): public currency exchange rates, downloaded without any user data. Attribution is shown in Settings > Exchange rates. Rates bundled with the app are used when offline.
- Apple StoreKit / App Store: the Moneyglot Pro auto-renewable subscription.
- Apple Foundation Models framework (Apple Intelligence, on-device): the AI savings plan. No data is sent to any server.
- GitHub Pages: hosts the privacy policy and support pages only.
The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. All user data stays on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in every region where it is offered. The only variation is the AI savings plan, which depends on Apple Intelligence being available for the device and its language and region settings; where it is not available, the smart insights are shown instead. The app is not offered in China mainland.

6. REGULATION AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Moneyglot is a personal budgeting tool. It does not provide banking, payments, lending, investing, trading or money-transfer services, does not connect to bank accounts, and does not give personalized investment advice; the AI suggestions are informational and labeled as not financial advice. No license is required. The only third-party content is public exchange-rate data, used under ExchangeRate-API's open-access terms with attribution.
