# Moneyglot

**Every currency. One clear picture.**

Moneyglot tracks your income, mortgage, car loan, credit card, bills, insurance and everyday spending in any of 160+ currencies — then shows you monthly, quarterly and yearly reports in the currency you choose.

## Support

- **Exchange rates look out of date?** Open *Settings › Exchange rates* and tap *Refresh now*. Rates update automatically about twice a day when you're online.
- **A repeating payment didn't appear?** Repeating entries are added when you open the app on or after the due date. Check *Settings › Repeating payments* to make sure the schedule isn't paused.
- **Restore Moneyglot Pro on a new iPhone:** *Settings › Restore purchases*.
- **Cancel Moneyglot Pro:** *Settings › Manage subscription*, or in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple Account › Subscriptions.
- **Export your data:** *Settings › Export all entries (CSV)*, or use the share button on any report.

Still stuck? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/asfateh/moneyglot/issues) and include your iOS version.
