# Moneyglot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Moneyglot is a personal income and expense tracker. It is designed so that **your financial data stays on your iPhone**.

## Data we collect

**None.** Moneyglot does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Data stored on your device

The entries, repeating payments and settings you create are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are included in your regular iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) under your own Apple Account, which Apple protects under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app, or using **Settings › Delete all data** inside the app, removes them.

## AI suggestions (Moneyglot Pro)

The AI savings plan is generated by Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence model. Your financial summary is processed **on your iPhone** and is not sent to us or to any third party.

## Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from [ExchangeRate-API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com). This request contains no personal or financial information — it only asks for the latest rates. Like any web request, it reveals your device's IP address to that service.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

## Children

Moneyglot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
